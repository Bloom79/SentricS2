## ⚡ 2. REAL-TIME 15-MINUTE MARKET TRADING (TIDE OPTIMIZATION)

### Market Opportunity

**TIDE Reform - Mandatory Starting January 1, 2025:**
- Shift from **hourly to 15-minute intervals** for imbalance settlement
- **Negative prices emerging** in Italy (especially Sardinia, Sicily)
- **Higher volatility** = more trading opportunities
- **Dynamic bidding zones** replacing static zones
- Revenue opportunities for **flexible assets and storage**

### What's Missing in SentricS2

❌ **No 15-minute interval optimization**
❌ **No real-time price monitoring**
❌ **No automated trading decisions**
❌ **No negative price alerts**
❌ **No curtailment strategies**

### Disruptive Functionality to Add

#### **Real-Time Energy Trading Platform**

**Core Features:**
1. **15-Minute Interval Optimization**
   - Real-time price monitoring (Terna/GME integration)
   - Automatic production/consumption scheduling
   - Load shifting recommendations
   - Generation curtailment automation
   - Storage charge/discharge timing

2. **Negative Price Management**
   - Automatic alerts for negative price periods
   - Curtailment triggers (stop production when not profitable)
   - Storage charging activation (get paid to charge)
   - Load increase recommendations (consume when paid)
   - PPA contract compliance checking

3. **Intraday Trading Automation**
   - Continuous trading in 15-minute blocks
   - Position optimization based on weather forecasts
   - Automatic bid placement
   - Portfolio balancing across multiple plants
   - Risk management and hedging

4. **Dynamic Zonal Pricing**
   - Zone-specific optimization
   - Congestion pricing awareness
   - Cross-zone arbitrage identification
   - Transmission constraint management

5. **Renewable Curtailment Intelligence**
   - Automatic curtailment during negative prices
   - PPA compatibility checking
   - Revenue impact forecasting
   - Regulatory compliance tracking
   - Historical performance analytics

**Integration Requirements:**
- **GME (Gestore Mercati Energetici)** API
- **Terna** real-time data feeds
- **Weather APIs** for production forecasting
- **IRIS (Italian Renewable Incentive System)** compliance

**Revenue Model:**
- Subscription: €200-1,000/month per plant
